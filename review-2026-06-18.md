# Review: owned-causality

## Verdict

**Reject, with an invitation to resubmit after the simulation is rebuilt.** The synthesis is erudite and the bibliography is genuine and largely well-used; as a conceptual essay assembling a non-circular, pre-personal primitive for agency, the paper is strong. But it presents itself as a *computable* theory whose "every numeric claim traces to a key in results.json," and the computation does not do what the prose says it does. The single most serious problem: of the four factors that are the paper's entire contribution, two are not implemented as defined (the directed-information factor drops the conditioning on `X_t, E` that is its whole point; the ΔV counterfactual uses an *independent noise stream*, so it measures noise-realisation differences rather than constraint timing — proven by the rock, which has zero constraints yet scores ΔV = 0.172), the key separation is hand-set rather than computed, and the §8 "handover" is tautological. The simulation confirms its own inputs.

## Claim grounding

- "Every numeric claim in the paper traces to a key in `simulation/output/results.json`." → **mostly supported, with exceptions.** The agency-ladder table (§7.1) matches the JSON to rounding. But two stated numbers do not trace cleanly (see Factual errors): the §8.2 "peak … at window 5" and the §8.2 "slightly longer ramp."

- "All rungs run for n = 8000 steps **under the same noise realisation**" (§7.1) → **unsupported / false.** `run_ladder` seeds each rung `seed + i` (`agency_ladder.py:226`), so every rung uses a *different* noise stream. The factor values are therefore not comparable across rungs in the way "same noise realisation" implies.

- "If ΔV_T is positive, the timing of the constraint deployment was necessary for survival" (§6.2) → **unsupported by the code, and contradicted by the results.** `_simulate_scrambled` (`agency_ladder.py:88`) replays with a fresh independent RNG (`seed + 31`), not the recorded noise. The `viability.py` docstring claims "identical noise"; the implementation does not deliver it. Direct evidence: the **rock has no constraints at all** (policy returns 0), so scrambling its all-zero constraint trajectory changes nothing — yet it scores ΔV = 0.172. That entire value is a noise-realisation artifact. ΔV does not measure what the paper says it measures.

- "$DI(U^{\Omega}\to Q \mid X_t, E_{t:t+T})$ … conditioned on the external trajectory, captures only the steering that genuinely originates inside the organisation" (§6.2) → **unsupported.** `directed_information_per_step` (`directed_info.py:80`) computes the *unconditioned* lagged mutual information $I(u_t; q_{t+1})$, with no conditioning on $X_t$ or $E$. The code's own docstring admits this. The conditioning that "kills the leaf in the wind" is never performed; it is sidestepped by making the external drive ~0 in the measurement window.

- "The ladder cleanly separates the two systems whose constraints are externally specified … from the four systems with internal constraint production" (§Abstract, §7) → **overstated.** The separation is entirely produced by the hand-set `maintained/required` fractions (`0/1, 0/2, 1/3, 2/3, 3/4, 4/4`), since persistence ≈ 1 for all rungs. The discriminating result is an input, not a finding.

- "directed information from endogenous constraints rises to a peak of 0.403 … the two traces crossing at window 6" (§Abstract, §8.2) → **supported by the JSON, but the result is built into the protocol** (see Argument). `di_omega_to_q[10] = 0.4032`, `crossover_index = 6` both check out.

- §8.3: "The formula reports $\mathfrak{O}_T \to 0$ for the organism over the post-scaffold window" (pathological case) → **unsupported.** No pathological run exists in `results.json`; this is asserted verbally as "the contrapositive," not computed.

## Argument

- **§7 (the ladder) — the headline separation is stipulated, not measured.** Γ reduces to the hand-coded closure fraction (persistence ≈ 1 everywhere). Proposition 1 ("Γ = 0 ⇒ 𝔒 = 0") is then trivially true of any product containing a zero factor, and the rock/thermostat = 0 result is a restatement of the modeller's choice to set their closure fraction to 0. The formula is not shown to *discover* ownership; it is shown to multiply by a number the author chose.

- **§7 — the "ladder" is non-monotonic and the paper under-discloses it.** 𝔒_T runs rock 0, thermostat 0, flame 4.0e‑5, **raf 2.2e‑5**, chemoton 2.1e‑4, **organism 1.8e‑4**. RAF (rung 4, closure 2/3) scores *below* flame (rung 3, closure 1/3), and organism (rung 6) below chemoton (rung 5). The paper admits chemoton > organism but never flags that a higher rung (RAF) is beaten by a lower one (flame). A metric called an "agency ladder" that does not climb monotonically with the ladder is weak evidence for the metric.

- **§6.2 / §7 — two of the four factors are not faithfully implemented** (DI unconditioned; ΔV noise-confounded, see Claim grounding). Since the paper's whole claim is "each of the four factors does work no other can," and two of them are not computing the defined quantity, the empirical demonstration of the four-factor structure does not stand.

- **§8 (scaffold handover) — the central result is tautological.** `scaffold.py` schedules `constraint_gate(t)` to ramp *up* starting exactly when the scaffold ramps *down* (both at `scaffold_until`). So $U^{\Omega}$ goes from off to on precisely as $E$ goes from on to off, *by construction*. The "crossover," the 10× inequality in Proposition 2, and the rising/falling DI traces are guaranteed by the input schedule, not discovered. This is not a model of handover; it is a plot of the schedule passed through a DI estimator. Proposition 2 is true but vacuous.

- **§8.1 — the interpretive leap is enormous and unargued.** A 1-D Langevin particle with a manually-gated drift term is offered as a model of "co-regulation, blessing, mentoring, trusted treatment, parental calm, coach confidence" and "guru capture." Nothing in the toy licenses that mapping; the toy was constructed to exhibit the crossover the metaphor wants.

- **§9 (the "I") — speculative, presented as continuous with the math.** The latent variable $S$ summarising 𝔒_T history is never computed anywhere. §9 is a (well-read) philosophical coda; framing it as the downstream of the formal object overstates the connection.

- **What is genuinely strong:** the central move — defining ownership without "I"/"self" on the right-hand side, via constraint closure plus a viability-gated, recursive influence — is a real and clarifying idea. The §2 elimination of "inside the boundary," "voluntary," "conscious," and "caused by an I" is crisp and persuasive. The integration of Rosen / Moreno–Mossio–Montévil / Juarrero / Deacon as "three vocabularies for one architecture" (§3.4) is the best part of the paper and would survive on its own.

## Factual errors

- **§8.2 / Proposition 2 proof: "peak $DI(U^{\text{ext}}\to Q)$ over all windows is 0.037 at window 5."** The JSON peak (0.03700) is at **window 7**; window 5 holds 0.02665. The value 0.037 is right; the window index is wrong. (The inequality still holds.)
- **§8.2: "during the scaffold-active windows (0 through 5), $DI(U^{\text{ext}}\to Q)$ runs between 0.003 and 0.037."** Within windows 0–5 the range is 0.0029–0.0267; the 0.037 occurs at window 7. Misstated range.
- **§8.2: "$DI(U^{\Omega}\to Q)$ stays at zero" during windows 0–5.** Window 5 is 0.00696, not zero.
- **§8.2: "the constraint loop activates linearly over a slightly longer ramp."** `constraint_ramp_steps = 2500` vs `scaffold_ramp_steps = 3000` (`run_all.py:36`): the constraint ramp is *shorter*, not longer.
- **§7.1: "under the same noise realisation."** False; per-rung seeds differ (see Claim grounding).

These are individually minor but they accumulate against the paper's explicit promise of full numerical traceability.

## Prose and insight

Alive: §1–§2 are genuinely good — the circularity diagnosis ("They explain agency by quietly assuming it") and the four-way elimination of folk meanings of "owned" are sharp and economical. §3.4's "three vocabularies, one architecture" is the paper's real intellectual contribution and reads with conviction. The recurring instances of the re-entry idea ("In a bacterium, chemotactic history changes receptor methylation … In a human, an act changes habit") land well.

Dead or inflated: §8.1's litany ("blessing, mentoring, trusted treatment, parental calm, coach confidence") is decoration draped over a toy that cannot bear it. §9 is competent but is essentially a reading list (Haggard, Bandura, Damasio, Dennett) lightly stitched to a latent variable that is never instantiated; it reads as forgettable survey rather than result. The propositions are dressed as theorems but one is trivial (Prop 1) and one is vacuous-by-construction (Prop 2); the `\begin{proposition}…\square` formatting lends a rigor the content doesn't have.

Genuine contribution: the *conceptual* primitive — owned causality as constraint-closed, viability-gated, recursive basin-steering, definable pre-personally — is real and worth publishing. The *formula and simulation* as currently built do not earn their starring role.

## Top fixes

1. **Implement the directed-information factor as defined, or change the definition.** Either compute a genuine conditional directed information $DI(U^{\Omega}\to Q \mid X_t, E)$ (condition on the state and external trajectory, as §6.2 and the Massey/Pearl citations promise), or stop claiming the factor "factors out external causes." As written, the code computes unconditioned lagged MI and the conditioning — the thing that distinguishes this from naïve "amount of internal control" — is absent.

2. **Fix the ΔV counterfactual to use the recorded noise.** Replay the scrambled-constraint trajectory under the *same* noise realisation as the endogenous run (the `viability.py` docstring already claims this). Re-run the ladder. Demonstrate that the rock and thermostat now get ΔV ≈ 0; if they don't, ΔV is not measuring constraint timing and the four-factor story needs rethinking.

3. **De-tautologise §8.** The handover is currently guaranteed by scheduling the constraint gate to switch on exactly as the scaffold switches off. Make the constraint loop *endogenously triggered* (e.g., activated by viability dropping, with no reference to the scaffold schedule), so that the crossover is a discovered consequence, not a replotted input. Otherwise drop the propositions and present §8 honestly as an illustration, not a result. Then — and only then — the §8.3 pathological case should be *simulated*, not asserted.

4. **Make Γ do real work, or stop calling the separation a finding.** The rock/thermostat = 0 result is entirely the hand-set closure fraction. Either compute the maintained-constraint fraction from the dynamics (so closure is measured, not stipulated), or state plainly that the O_T = 0 boundary is definitional and that the simulation tests only the *non-trivial* factors. Soften "the formula discriminates …" accordingly.

5. **Address the non-monotonic ladder.** Disclose that RAF scores below flame and organism below chemoton, and either explain why a "ladder" metric needn't be monotonic (and what it should be monotonic in) or fix the policies/estimator so 𝔒_T tracks the closure ordering. Report variability across seeds (means ± SD over many noise realisations) instead of a single realisation whose ordering the paper itself calls "noisy."

6. **Correct the numerical/prose slips** in §7.1 and §8.2: the "same noise realisation" claim, the "peak at window 5" (it is window 7), the "between 0.003 and 0.037" range for windows 0–5, the "stays at zero" at window 5, and the "slightly longer ramp" (it is shorter).

7. **Right-size §8.1 and §9.** Cut or heavily caveat the psychotherapy/mentoring/guru mapping unless a model that can support it is built; recast §9 explicitly as interpretation, not as the downstream of a computed latent variable, since no $S$ is ever computed.

8. **Minor citation hygiene.** Either justify or soften the "noise channel of identical capacity" gloss on Kolchinsky & Wolpert, whose actual construction is correlation-scrambling counterfactuals.
