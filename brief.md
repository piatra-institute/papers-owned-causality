## Question

What is the right formal content of the claim *my action changed my future
state*? The primitive for agency must be definable without "I" or "self" on the
right-hand side, on pain of circularity.

## Claim

A causal influence is **owned** when a persistent organisation produces the
constraints that generate it, those constraints steer the organisation's future
attractor regime, and the resulting regime feeds back into the production of
further constraints. Owned causality is a product of four factors: closure of
constraints; directed information from endogenous perturbation to future
macrostate; viability gain over a scrambled-constraint counterfactual; and mutual
information from achieved macrostate to future constraints. The "I" appears later
as a compressed model of recurrent high-owned-causality history.

## Cornerstone literature

Must engage: chemical reaction-network theory (Feinberg), chemical organisation
theory (Dittrich & Speroni di Fenizio), autocatalytic-set / RAF theory (Kauffman,
Hordijk & Steel), closure to efficient causation (Rosen, Hofmeyr), closure of
constraints (Moreno, Mossio, Montévil), autopoiesis (Maturana & Varela), semantic
closure (Pattee, Hoffmeyer, Kolchinsky & Wolpert), Markov-blanket inference
(Friston, Kirchhoff et al.), basin dynamics (Lorenz), circular causality (Haken).
